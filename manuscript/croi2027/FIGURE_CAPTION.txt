Analytic predictions and independent Monte Carlo validation. Rows give five exact-cancellation conditions and single-condition failures; the dashed line marks no bias. Monte Carlo means over 24 replicates of 15 million individuals; 95% intervals are narrower than the symbols; standardised discrepancies appear at right. Losses and returns cancel exactly at 1.000. Absorbing mortality attenuates the estimate; acquisition in temporarily unobservable states attenuates below eta=1 and inflates above it, so bias direction is set by acquisition hazard, not occupancy. All seven comparisons agree within |t|=1.22 on 23 df; simulator and derivation share no code.
