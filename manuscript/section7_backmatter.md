## Use of artificial intelligence

Claude (Anthropic) was used throughout this work: analytic derivation, the simulation and figure code, the empirical parameterisation, and drafting. All analytic results were checked against an independently written generative simulator sharing no code with the derivation; every quantitative claim in this manuscript is regenerated by the accompanying repository and asserted by its test suite; and the full derivation, implementation and audit record are public.

This is disclosed in full rather than minimally. The work's claim to credibility is that it is checkable, and an undisclosed tool is the one part of it a reader cannot check. The author is responsible for the content and for its correctness.

## Data and code availability

All analysis code, the test suite, and every generated table and figure are available at **github.com/Nyx-Dynamics/nyx-eligibility-dynamics** under the MIT licence. The repository regenerates every quantitative claim in this manuscript from source: `make verify` runs the theorem-level and simulation tests, `make figures` regenerates all tables and figures, and `make tex` rebuilds this document. It also carries the audit record documenting the analysis in full.

Two classes of input are not redistributed. The **CEPHIA public-use dataset** (doi:10.5281/zenodo.4900634) is required for the empirical recency function; tests and figures depending on it skip cleanly when it is absent, and retrieval instructions are in the repository. **Publisher articles and supplements** consulted during the work are likewise not redistributed.

Statistical tables from the Bureau of Justice Statistics **are** redistributed within the repository, as US Government works in the public domain, so that the in-custody mortality parameters can be checked against their sources rather than against a summary.
