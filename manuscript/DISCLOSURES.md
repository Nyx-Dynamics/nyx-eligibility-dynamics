# Declarations

Drafted for a preprint posting. **Three items need the author's confirmation and are marked ⚠.**

## Use of artificial intelligence

Claude (Anthropic) was used throughout: analytic derivation, the simulation and figure code, the empirical parameterisation, and drafting. All analytic results were checked against an independently written generative simulator sharing no code with the derivation, every quantitative claim is regenerated by the accompanying repository and asserted by its test suite, and the full derivation, implementation and audit record are public. The author is responsible for the content and for its correctness.

This is disclosed in full rather than minimally. The work's claim to credibility is that it is checkable, and an undisclosed tool is the one part a reader cannot check.

The predecessor analysis that this work supersedes — manuscript QAIV24714, *Calibration-to-Deployment Mismatch in HIV Prevention Trials: How Structural Censoring Biases Counterfactual Incidence Estimates*, submitted to *JAIDS* in May 2026 and declined after external review — has its software repository publicly archived at Zenodo, **doi:10.5281/zenodo.20344293** (v9.0.0, 22 May 2026, CC-BY-4.0). **Its central empirical conclusions are not carried forward.** The present work corrects and supersedes them, and that correction is the reason this paper exists; §5.8 gives the technical account.

## Data availability

All analysis code and every generated table are in the repository above. Two classes of input are not redistributed:

- the **CEPHIA public-use dataset** (Zenodo doi:10.5281/zenodo.4900634), which the empirical recency fit requires. Tests and figures depending on it skip cleanly when absent;
- **publisher PDFs and supplements** consulted during the work.

**BJS statistical tables are redistributed**, under `data/bjs/`, as US Government works in the public domain.
